---

## 🚀 What is QRForge?

QRForge is a privacy-focused QR code toolkit built with pure HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

It allows you to create, customize, scan, download and print QR codes without sending your data to any server.

> 🔒 Everything happens locally on your device.

No account.  
No server.  
No API.  
No tracking.  
No uploads.

## 📷 QR Scanner

Scan QR codes in multiple ways:

### Webcam Scanner
Use your device camera to scan QR codes instantly.

### Image Scanner
Upload an existing QR code image and QRForge will decode it locally.

Your camera data and uploaded images are never sent anywhere.

## 🔐 Privacy First

Privacy is one of the main goals of QRForge.

QRForge does **not**:

- ❌ Upload your QR data
- ❌ Store your information on a server
- ❌ Track your activity
- ❌ Require an account
- ❌ Use cloud processing

All QR generation and scanning is processed directly inside your browser.

## 📱 Install as an App

QRForge is also a Progressive Web App (PWA).

### Android

1. Open QRForge in Chrome.
2. Tap the browser menu `⋮`.
3. Select **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
4. QRForge will behave like a normal app.

### Desktop

Open QRForge in a supported browser and select:

**Install QRForge**

from the browser's install option.
